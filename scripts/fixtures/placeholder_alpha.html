Placeholder Alpha is a town known for its windmill. It was first described in 1742 by a travelling scholar, who counted 48,213 inhabitants and over 3,500 windmills.[2]

Today the town covers about 1,250 square kilometres and is visited by roughly 1.2 million people every year, most of them in the 3rd quarter.

History

[edit]

Early settlers arrived in the 14th century. The town grew quickly after 1742, when a railway connected it to the coast. A great fire in 1911 destroyed nearly half of its windmills.

Geography

The climate is mild, with temperatures between 12 and 31 °C. Flag: national flag.
