Season 19

Bigg Boss 20, also known as Bigg Boss: Tathas-two, is the twentieth season of the Indian Hindi-language reality television series Bigg Boss. It premiered on 6 September 2026 on Colors TV and streams digitally on JioHotstar.[1] Salman Khan is hosting the show for the seventeenth consecutive season.[2][3]

Rohit Shetty guest hosted the Weekend Ka Vaar for the seventh week in Khan's absence.[4]

Production

Development and theme

The season's tagline is “Tathas-two”, introducing a “vardaan” that gives contestants a second chance in the game. The concept is built around the English wordplay “that's two”.[5]

Promotions and release

On 20 July 2026, JioHotstar announced that the twentieth season of Bigg Boss Hindi, hosted by Salman Khan, would premiere in September 2026 on Colors TV and JioHotstar as part of a network initiative to air six Bigg Boss regional editions simultaneously.[6][7]

The official first look and logo were unveiled on 21 July 2026.[8][9] This was followed by a series of teasers released in early August 2026, which confirmed the 6 September 2026 premiere date and introduced the season's “Tathas-two” theme.[10][11][12][13] A full-length trailer expanding on the gameplay mechanics was released on 12 August 2026.[14][15]

On 4 September 2026, JioHotstar and Colors TV released promotional clips featuring confirmed contestants Isha Rikhi and Kanika Mann.[16][17] On 5 September 2026, a teaser featuring rapper Yung DSA was released, in which he performed his song Yeda Yung.[18] On the same day, actor-politician Manoj Tiwari appeared alongside his daughter Rhiti Tiwari in a promotional segment to introduce her as a contestant.[19][20]

Casting

On 25 August 2026, JioHotstar and Colors TV announced the return of the audience-voting initiative Fans Ka Faisla (Fans' Decision), previously used in the nineteenth season. Through the JioHotstar app, viewers were given the opportunity to vote for one of two potential contestants to enter the house prior to the premiere. The voting period was scheduled from 25 August to 2 September 2026.[21][22] The two candidates were Punjabi actress Love Gill and Model Rhiya Ahir.[23][24]

Eye logo

The season's logo features a stylized eye with a multicolored geometric iris (in shades of purple, blue, red, orange, and teal) converging on a golden center. It is accompanied by a metallic gold outline and a matching "Bigg Boss" wordmark. According to the network, the design is intended to reflect the season's "Tathas-two" theme, symbolizing manifestation and destiny.[25][26]

Broadcast

Following the release strategy of the previous season, the show prioritizes digital streaming, with daily episodes airing on JioHotstar at 9:00 pm IST, followed by a television broadcast on Colors TV at 10:30 pm IST.[27]

House

The House featured a colourful "Wonderland" theme inspired by the concept of duality, incorporating surrealist fantasy and animal-inspired elements throughout. Designed by Varsha Jain, the house covers approximately 10,000 square feet and features 104 cameras.[28]

  • Living area: Features a large central sofa, a 12-seater dining table, parachute-inspired seating, and a pink teapot installation used as a throne.[29]
  • Kitchen: Designed as the "BB Food Truck", featuring a food-truck-inspired kitchen setup.[30]
  • Bedroom: Contains 17 beds, with animal-inspired decorations including a scorpion and chameleon, along with mushroom-shaped wall elements.[31]
  • Bathroom: Features an upside-down neon-pink rabbit installation, seating areas, and suspended chimpanzee decorations.[32]
  • Garden/Outdoor area: Features a shark-themed swimming pool, a floral tap installation, a snake-wrapped tree used as seating, and various animal sculptures.[33]
  • Room 20: A new room introduced for the twentieth season, replacing the Assembly Room from the previous season. This is the Wall of Fame of the Bigg Boss house, a room containing photos of all the contestants. Initially, each contestant's photo is displayed in split faces. After a contestant loses their lifeline, any contestant flips their photo, revealing a single, complete picture of the contestant.[34]
  • Room 2X: A mysterious room featuring two differently sized doors; the room were mostly used to either take 1 lifeline from one or multiple contestants by pressing the X Buzzer or give 1 lifeline to a contestant which has single lifeline by pressing the 2 number buzzer.[33]

Additional features include numerous three-dimensional animal and fantasy-inspired installations, with recurring eye motifs representing Bigg Boss's surveillance.[35]

Housemates

The following housemates entered the house in order:[36]

Original entrants

  • Kanika Mann – Actress known for Guddan Tumse Na Ho Payega, Fear Factor Khatron ke Khiladi 12 and Naagin 7.[37]
  • Aasif Khan – Actor known for Panchayat.[38]
  • Qazi Ahmad Touqeer – Singer known for Fame Gurukul.[39]
  • Isha Rikhi – Actress and ex-wife of rapper Badshah.[40]
  • Aamrapali Dubey – Actress primarily known for her work in Bhojpuri cinema.[41]
  • Rohed Khan – Model and actor.[42]
  • Lovepreet Kaur Gill – Actress known for her work in Punjabi cinema.[43]
  • Yung DSA – Rapper known for his track "Yeda Yung".[44]
  • Mary Kom – Retired amateur boxer and former Member of Parliament. Known for her achievements in boxing, including winning a bronze medal at the 2012 Summer Olympics.[45]
  • Tanmay "Sc0utOP" Singh – E-sports player and streamer.[46]
  • Arishfa Khan – Actress and social media influencer.[47]
  • Kushal "Gullu" Tanwar – Reality show personality known for Roadies XX, Splitsvilla X6 and Maa Hai Na.[48]
  • Aman Gandhi – Actor known for Bhagya Lakshmi and Kyunki Rishton Ke Bhi Roop Badalte Hain.[49]
  • Uditi Singh – Actress.
  • Rhiti Tiwari – Singer, songwriter and daughter of Bigg Boss 4 contestant Manoj Tiwari.[50]
  • Mahhi Vij – Actress known for Laagi Tujhse Lagan and Seher – Hone Ko Hai. She is also known for Jhalak Dikhlaa Jaa 4, Nach Baliye 5 and Khatron Ke Khiladi 7.[51]

Wild card entrants

  • Manish Goel - Actor known for his roles in popular daily soaps including Kahaani Ghar Ghar Kii, Kasautii Zindagii Kay, Bhabhi and Anupamaa. He is also known for Nach Baliye 1 and Welcome – Baazi Mehmaan Nawazi Ki.[52]
  • Nakul Dhull - Social media personality.[53]

Twists

Fans Ka Faisla

On 25 August 2026, JioHotstar and Colors TV announced the return of the Fans Ka Faisla campaign, previously introduced in the nineteenth season, allowing viewers to select one housemate between Rhiya Ahir and Love Gill via the JioHotstar app from 25 August to 2 September 2026.[54]

At the grand premiere, host Salman Khan declared that Love Gill was selected to enter the house, while Rhiya Ahir was eliminated.[55]

Tathas-two

Each housemate entered the season with two lives, losing one life (trans. Jeevandan) upon eviction or through various twists.[56]

Twists
  • Upon entering the house, Kanika was asked by Bigg Boss to observe the housemates and select the one she disliked the most. On Day 2, when asked by Bigg Boss, she chose Qazi, who was subsequently barred from participating in the captaincy task.
  • On Day 3, Bigg Boss asked the contenders for captaincy, Aasif, Gullu and Yung DSA, to name a housemate whose lifeline they would want to take if they became Captain. They chose Arishfa, Uditi, and Kanika respectively.
Nominations Arishfa, Kanika, Love and Uditi were nominated for the first week eviction.
House captain None
Tasks
  • Nomination Task: On Day 3, housemates were asked to nominate two other housemates for the upcoming eviction.
  • Happy Birthday, Bigg Boss: On Day 2, the contestants became pieces of a cake structure. When the buzzer sounded, they had to sit on the plate while a birthday song was played. Once the song ended, they had to return to the cake structure and align themselves in the empty cells. The last three housemates to return were eliminated from each round. Three housemates were to be selected as nominees for House Captain. Qazi could not participate in the task & became the coordinator.
  • Bigg Boss Movie Theatre Captaincy Task: On Day 4, the captaincy contenders, Aasif, Gullu, and Yung DSA, were seated on the movie theatre chairs with popcorn buckets placed in front of them with Mahhi as the coordinator of the task. The remaining housemates had to collect popcorn in the popcorn boxes placed on their heads from the popcorn machine when the task began and put them into the bucket of the contender they wanted to make the Captain. The contenders also had to protect their buckets from the other housemates.
Results
  • Happy Birthday, Bigg Boss: Aasif, Gullu and Yung DSA won the task and became contenders for next week's captaincy.
  • Bigg Boss Movie Theatre Captaincy Task: Yung DSA won the task and became the first Captain.

Nomination notes

  • ^1: The Fans Ka Faisla campaign allowed viewers to select one housemate between Rhiya Ahir and Love Gill via the JioHotstar app from 25 August to 2 September 2026. At the grand premiere, host Salman Khan declared that Love Gill was selected to enter the house, while Rhiya Ahir was eliminated.
  • ^2: On Day 6, Uditi was evicted through public voting and lost her lifeline.
  • ^3: On Day 8, following weekly nomination activity, Bigg Boss cancelled the housemates nominations and directly nominated Aasif, Rohed and Scout for violating house rules by discussing the nominations.
  • ^4: On Day 11, Aasif, Rohed and Scout lost their lifelines after Kanika, Gullu and Mahhi respectively took them to compete for the captaincy. Meanwhile, Rhiti failed, resulting in the cancellation of her captaincy nomination.
  • ^5: On Day 15, Aman took Yung DSA’s lifeline to avail luxury ration for himself.
  • ^6: On Day 16, Isha and Rhiti lost their lifeline after finishing in the bottom two in the ranking activity among housemates with lifelines.
  • ^7: On Day 18, Aasif’s captaincy nomination cancelled after receiving most rejection by housemates. Furthermore, the remaining nominees except Gullu had to mutually give three names for upcoming captaincy. They choose Mary, Love and Rhiti.
  • ^8: On Day 26, Rhiti was evicted by captaincy nominees Mahhi, Uditi and Yung DSA.
  • ^9: On Day 28, Mary was evicted through public voting and lost her lifeline.
  • ^10: On Day 32, Gullu’s captaincy nomination were cancelled by majority of housemates.
  • ^11: On Day 35, Aman was evicted through public voting and lost his lifeline.
  • ^12: On Day 38, Nakul was ejected due to physical violence against Gullu.
  • ^13: On Day 49, Manish was evicted through public voting and lost his lifeline.
  • ^14: On Day 51, House Captain Mahhi was given the power to place housemates (evict/save) on a Ludo board as part of the weekly nomination process. She then spun a dice wheel and invited a housemate per turn to swap the positions of other housemates on the board.
  • ^15: On Day 52, Mahhi’s captaincy nomination cancelled after receiving most rejection by housemates.
  • ^16: On Day 65, Bigg Boss removed Arishfa from captaincy a day before her tenure after most housemates were seen violating the rules by sleeping. She was also barred from participating in the next week’s captaincy task.
  • ^17: On Day 66, following the physical assault on Yung DSA, Bigg Boss punished Scout by nominating him for the entire season, permanently disqualifying him from becoming captain, barring him from receiving any special powers (such as a lifeline), and making him liable for immediate eviction for any further violation of house rules.
