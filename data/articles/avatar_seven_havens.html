Avatar: The Last Airbender franchise

Avatar: Seven Havens is an American animated fantasy television series created by Michael Dante DiMartino and Bryan Konietzko for Paramount+. It serves as a sequel series to Avatar: The Last Airbender (2005–2008) and The Legend of Korra (2012–2014). It follows twin Avatar sisters Pavi and Nisha as they uncover their mysterious origins and protect the Seven Havens—cities that stand as the last strongholds of civilization after a global apocalypse seemingly caused by Korra, their predecessor.

The principal voice cast includes Saheli Khan as Avatar Pavi, alongside Aishu Devan, Akshay Khanna, Major Curda, Sakina Jaffrey, Darren Barnet, Dianne Doan, and Dee Bradley Baker. Creatives from both The Last Airbender and The Legend of Korra returned, including Jeremy Zuckerman creating original music and Ethan Spaulding, who was a story artist and director on both shows, executive producing. The series is produced by Nickelodeon Animation and Avatar Studios, and La Chouette Compagnie provides animation.

The first season of Avatar: Seven Havens, titled Book One: Survival, premiered on October 9, 2026, on Paramount+, with the concurrent release of its first three episodes. The other ten episodes were released weekly in batches of three to four episodes through October 30. It received acclaim from critics, who complimented the characters, storytelling, and the visual style. With three additional seasons also ordered, Seven Havens is expected to conclude with its fourth season.

Setting

[edit]

Background

[edit]
Further information: Avatar: The Last Airbender and The Legend of Korra

Avatar: Seven Havens is set in the fictional world of Avatar: The Last Airbender and The Legend of Korra where people previously belonged to five nations: the Northern and the Southern Water Tribes, the Earth Kingdom, the Fire Nation, the monastic Air Nomads, and the United Republic of Nations. The distinguishing aspect of the series is "bending", the ability of some people to telekinetically manipulate one of the four classical elements associated with their nation or heritage (water, earth, fire, or air). Bending is carried out by spiritual and physical exercises. The Avatar is the only person who can bend all four elements and is tasked with maintaining peace and balance in the world, using their powers and wisdom. The Avatar is constantly reincarnated and reborn.

Premise

[edit]

Seven Havens begins 225 years after the time of Korra, the previous Avatar. The world has become mostly inhospitable after a cataclysm terraformed it into the perennially calamitous Harshlands. With Korra blamed for the cataclysm, the world has come to view the Avatar as an annihilator instead of as a savior. The only refuges from the Harshlands are the eponymous Seven Havens, isolated hubs of civilization created in the wake of the disaster to save humanity. In Ellora, one of the havens, 9-year-old orphaned twin sisters, Pavi and Nisha, discover that they are the Avatar, marking the first time two Avatars have existed simultaneously. Hunted by both human enemies and spirit anomalies, the twins must uncover their strange shared backstory and save the Seven Havens from looming threats.[1][6][7]

Production

[edit]

Development

[edit]

In 2018, Netflix announced that a live-action remake of Avatar: The Last Airbender (2005–2008) was to start production in 2019.[15][16] The series' original creators, Michael Dante DiMartino and Bryan Konietzko, were initially announced to be the executive producers and showrunners.[17][18] In June 2020, the creators departed the series due to creative differences.[19] That same year, Nickelodeon licensed the original The Last Airbender as well as The Legend of Korra (2012–2014) to Netflix, which led to a boom in popularity for both series. This success convinced Nickelodeon to rekindle their relationship with Konietzko and DiMartino and explore further opportunities.[20][21]

In February 2021, ViacomCBS announced during its annual Investor Day the formation of Avatar Studios, a new division of Nickelodeon dedicated to the creation of projects based on animated television series Avatar: The Last Airbender and The Legend of Korra. The series creators and executive producers Konietzko and DiMartino would serve as co-chief creative officers.[22][23] In late 2024, Knight Edge Media reported that a new Avatar series following the story of the Avatar after Korra was in production at Avatar Studios. The report coincided with leaked plot details and production materials across social media.[24][25][26] According to the leaks, the series would star 9-year-old twin earthbender sisters, with one of them being the Avatar.[26]

In February 2025, the series, titled Avatar: Seven Havens, was officially announced for a two-season and 26-episode pick-up at Nickelodeon to coincide with the original series' 20th anniversary. DiMartino and Konietzko were reported to be creators and executive producers on the new series. Executive producing were Ethan Spaulding and Sehaj Sethi.[27][28][29] Nickelodeon Animation and Avatar Studios produce.[9] A first look image of the series debuted at San Diego Comic-Con in July 2025.[30] Alexandra Chiu was the supervising director for the series.[31]

On October 9, 2026, the same day as the series premiere, at New York Comic-Con, the series was renewed for a third and a fourth season.[32] DiMartino confirmed the same month that Seven Havens would conclude after its fourth season;[33] the third and fourth seasons were already in production by the airing of the first season.[34]

Casting

[edit]

In July 2026, along with the first trailer, it was revealed that the voice cast would feature Saheli Khan as Avatar Pavi, alongside Aishu Devan, Akshay Khanna, Major Curda, Sakina Jaffrey, Darren Barnet, Dianne Doan, and Dee Bradley Baker in starring roles and supporting roles.[9]

Animation, design and music

[edit]

The animation is provided by La Chouette Compagnie, a French animation studio.[35] According to DiMartino and Konietzko, the series' art direction was influenced by French surrealist artist Mœbius and 1990s anime.[30] Avatar: Seven Havens music was composed by Jeremy Zuckerman, who previously scored Avatar: The Last Airbender and The Legend of Korra with Benjamin Wynn.[36][37]

Release

[edit]

Avatar: Seven Havens premiered on October 9, 2026, on Paramount+ with its first three episodes.[9][13] The rest of the season was released in three to four episode batches from October 16 to 30.[13] Originally set to broadcast in 2027,[27] it was announced during San Diego Comic-Con 2026 that series would premiere earlier on Paramount+,[9][38][39] on the same day that the Avatar Aang: The Last Airbender film was originally scheduled to release.[40][41][42] The series was initially ordered for two seasons, or "books", of thirteen episodes each;[36][27][9] the first was titled Survival.[43]

Reception

[edit]

On the review aggregator website Rotten Tomatoes, the series holds an approval rating of 100%, based on 21 reviews, with an average of rated reviews of 8.1/10. The website's critics consensus reads, "Seven Havens embarks on a new saga with inspired storytelling, a vibrant sense of style, and remarkable characters that ultimately usher in a positive new era for Avatar."[44] Metacritic, which uses a weighted average, assigned a score of 80 out of 100 based on 11 critics, indicating "generally favorable" reviews.[45]
