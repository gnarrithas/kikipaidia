Series 1

The second series of The Celebrity Traitors began airing on BBC One on 1 October 2026, with Claudia Winkleman presenting the ten-episode series.[1][2] The companion show The Celebrity Traitors: Uncloaked has also returned, hosted by Ed Gamble.[3] In the series, 21 celebrities competed to win up to £100,000 for their chosen charities.[2]

The series concluded on 30 October, 2026, when Romesh Ranganathan and Sebastian Croft won as Faithfuls, sharing the prize money for their chosen charities, the Refugee Council and Choose Love respectively.[4]

Production

In November 2025, following the first series (the finale of which was the most viewed television broadcast in the UK in 2025),[5] it was announced that The Celebrity Traitors had been renewed for a second series.[3][6] Produced by Studio Lambert, the series began filming in May 2026 at Ardross Castle and had concluded by June.[7][8][9] Soon after being announced, the celebrities arrived at the nearby Inverness Airport.[10] During filming, the cast stayed at the Inverness Airport Courtyard by Marriott hotel.[11]

Prior to the official cast announcement, many celebrities expressed interest in appearing on the show, including Amanda Holden, Tom Hiddleston and Danny Dyer, who had turned down appearing on the first series. However, none of these celebrities were cast.[12] Despite this, in March 2026 host Claudia Winkleman hinted that "the cast is extraordinary".[13]

A teaser trailer was released simultaneously with the cast announcement on 2 May 2026, depicting the contestants' names on Traitors slates at an airport; some were misspelt, including "Richardy Grant", "Miranda Heart", and "Rob Bucket" (a reference to the series, in which players often spelt names incorrectly as they voted at the Round Table).[2] A first-look trailer featuring the celebrities was released on 27 August 2026.[14] Speaking at the Edinburgh TV Festival in August 2026, BBC content chief Kate Phillips said that the series will feature "tears and surprises".[14] On 15 September, the series was confirmed to begin on 1 October, with episodes airing on Thursdays and Fridays on BBC One at 8pm. A special programme titled Traitor or Faithful? Claudia Decides was released on BBC iPlayer after the first episode aired.[15]

Contestants

The 21 celebrities competing in the series were revealed on 2 May 2026.[7][16][17] The Guardian dubbed the line-up "one of the most high-profile casts ever assembled for a reality TV show".[18] Significant pre-existing relationships between celebrities include the close friendship of comedians Rob Beckett and Romesh Ranganathan,[16][19] who have appeared together on Rob & Romesh Vs;[2] Ranganathan and Julie Hesmondhalgh, who are the presenter and narrator respectively on The Weakest Link;[19] while Jerry Hall and Richard E. Grant are close friends and neighbours.[20] Their chosen charities were not revealed until after the show had concluded, with the winner's charity named in the final.

101"Episode 1"1 October 2026 (2026-10-01)15.41

After the contestants meet in the Traitors Church, Julie is deemed by the group as the most trustworthy and given two shields to distribute – she chooses to keep one for herself and gives the other to Joe. At the Round Table, Claudia selects Maya and Richard as Traitors, but warns the players that the Traitor team is not yet complete. When asked by Joe, Richard states he is a Traitor, startling the Faithfuls. After meeting in the turret for the first time, the Traitors are informed that they must summon two players tomorrow, with one becoming a Traitor and the other being murdered.

The next day, the players succeed in their first mission, earning the full £15,000. Ross is suspicious of being murdered in plain sight by Bella – who placed a captain's hat on his head during the mission – and Leigh-Anne. Amol and James A. question Richard about his offhand comment, but Richard deflects by claiming he was joking and just disappointed. The Traitors summon Amol and James A. to the church, where they each plead their case to be recruited. As Amol and James A. lie in coffins, Maya and Richard make their decision.[25]
112"Episode 2"2 October 2026 (2026-10-02)14.95

The Traitors choose to recruit James A. and murder Amol. Some players suspect Ross as being recruited after he was not murdered, which Maya encourages. Julie considers James A. as a potential recruit, Sebastian thinks Maya would be a good Traitor, and other players are still suspicious of Richard, who is asked many times about his statement that he was a Traitor the day before. James A. is nervous as a Traitor and states he cannot defend Richard too much without outing himself. The players form pairs ahead of the mission.

For the graveyard mission, the Traitors select Ross (and, by extension, his partner Romesh) to begin on the surface. Alongside banking £5,000, the players succeed in digging everyone up except Jerry and Hannah (due to Jerry quitting early), leaving them as the only players up for murder. At the Round Table, suspicions fall on Julie (as choosing her as the most trustworthy could have been the players inadvertently picking a Traitor), Leigh-Anne (for touching up her make-up before leaving the castle), and Richard. While Joanne and Jerry defend him, pressure grows on Richard before the vote is held.[26]
123"Episode 3"8 October 2026 (2026-10-08)15.36

Richard is banished by a near unanimous vote (including both Maya and James A. voting for him). Because Myha'la defended Richard, reneged on her argument by voting for him, and winked at him when she put her slate up, she falls under suspicion. Instead of murdering, the Traitors decide to seduce Hannah and she accepts their offer, joining them in the turret. The next day, the Faithfuls are stunned when both Jerry and Hannah enter breakfast, but surmise that a recruitment has happened. Miranda immediately suspects Hannah, while others suspect Jerry.

During the mission, Myha'la and Hannah compete for a shield which Myha'la takes. Sharon and Maya also collect shields but the last gold chest requires two to be sacrificed. After some hesitation, Myha'la and Sharon give up their shields and the full £6,000 is banked. At the Round Table, suspicion primarily falls on Jerry (for not acknowledging her and Hannah's survival at breakfast) and Myha'la (for switching her vote). Myha'la is banished and reveals herself as a Faithful. After the round table, Miranda states she is certain Hannah is a recruited Traitor. The Traitors reconvene and discuss who to murder, settling on either James B., Bella, or Sebastian.[27]
134"Episode 4"9 October 2026 (2026-10-09)15.05

The Traitors choose to murder Bella, surprising the Faithful and increasing tension in the group. Julie's strong reaction makes some players suspicious, while Miranda continues to doubt Hannah. Jerry suspects James A. but is not taken seriously. In the mission, Sebastian and Kenny win shields as the group earns £4,500. Hannah hesitates when given an opportunity to take a shield, making Kenny wary of her.

Joanne and James B. become convinced Julie is a Traitor, while Rob agrees with Miranda's doubts about Hannah but wants to vote for Julie first. At the Round Table, suspicion focuses on Hannah's changed behaviour and Julie being chosen as the most trustworthy. Despite support from Romesh and Hannah, Julie is banished by a close vote and reveals she is a Faithful, shifting suspicion onto James B.

That night, Claudia tells the Traitors they will not murder normally but instead choose four players to play hide-and-seek in crypts. They select Rob, Ross, Leigh‑Anne, and Romesh, before learning they will be murdering face-to-face. The chosen four discover that one crypt can be locked with a key, protecting them from murder. The others decide Leigh‑Anne should have the key before running and hiding in their crypts to await the Traitors.[28]
145"Episode 5"15 October 2026 (2026-10-15)15.29

The Traitors murder Rob face-to-face. At breakfast, the Faithfuls are surprised by the decision and suspicions fall on Ross as a potential Traitor among the four hide-and-seek players. Other suspicions fall on Hannah and James B., while Sebastian is suspicious of James A. In the mission, the players bank the full £8,000. As part of the mission, six players must be sacrificed and kept isolated until the Round Table. James A., Romesh, Maya, Jerry, Joanne, and Hannah are sacrificed. Maya realises that the Traitors have unwittingly made a mistake, with all three of them now unable to influence the Faithfuls in the castle.

At the Round Table, suspicions predominantly fall on Hannah, led by Miranda, Sebastian, and Kenny. She is banished by a near unanimous vote, with both other Traitors – Maya and James A. – voting for her. She reveals she is a Traitor. Afterwards, some suspicion falls on Joanne after she unintentionally implied she had been at the face-to-face murder. Joe openly begs for the Traitors to recruit him now that a spot is available. That night, the Traitors discuss recruiting either Joanne, Kenny, or Joe. They choose Kenny, who accepts the offer.[29]
156"Episode 6"16 October 2026 (2026-10-16)15.53

The next day, the Faithful realise that a recruitment has happened. Suspicions immediately land on Joe, while others suspect Joanne after a gaffe at the Round Table. In the mission, the players bank £7,500 and Maya earns the shield. Jerry was voted ‘The One With Their Head in the Clouds’, Romesh was voted as having ‘The Best Poker Face’, and Michael was voted ‘Leader of the Round Table’ – which worries him that he is being too prominent. Ross and Leigh-Anne suspect each other after the hide-and-seek murder.

At the Round Table, suspicions fall on Joanne (for her slip-up and chaotic voting record), Ross (for constantly being picked for special roles by the Traitors), and Joe (for openly wanting to be a Traitor, and suggesting the Traitors would be forced to recruit if he left, unintentionally implying he was a Traitor). Joe is banished in a near-unanimous vote and reveals he is a Faithful. Afterwards, the Traitors are told they must murder in plain sight by spraying a perfume and getting someone to yawn in the same room. Kenny sprays the perfume but later leaves the room as Maya and James A. try to get Ross and Jerry to yawn.[30]
167"Episode 7"22 October 2026 (2026-10-22)15.50

Ross yawns and is murdered in plain sight. The next day, Michael suspects Leigh-Anne had been distracting the Faithful when she taught Miranda a dance the night before. Kenny tries to plant the idea that Sebastian killed Ross in the bar. During the mission, the Traitors attempt to sneakily form a shield to bank money while the Faithful are blindfolded. James A. does not even lift his blindfold, frustrating Maya and Kenny. Despite making some progress, Maya and Kenny ultimately become nervous about being heard and fail to complete the shield, so no money is banked. Four players earn shields.

Miranda suspects Joanne and Maya of moving around during the mission, which Joanne refutes. Sharon remembers James A. theorising the use of the perfume bottles in a murder in plain sight when he was still a Faithful. The players discuss this theory, but decide Ross would have noticed and mentioned it.

At the Round Table, suspicions fall on Leigh-Anne, Joanne, Sebastian, and Miranda, but nobody feels confident sharing their theories. Jerry is eventually banished for causing chaos and randomly throwing out names, and she reveals that she is a Faithful. After the Round Table, James B. suspects Sharon. In the turret, Kenny and Maya question James A. about refusing to help in the challenge and then supporting Sharon's correct perfume theory. The Traitors decide to murder either Miranda, Romesh, or Sebastian.[31]
178"Episode 8"23 October 2026 (2026-10-23)15.30

The Traitors choose to murder Miranda. Suspicions fall on Joanne after she and Miranda accused one another, and Sharon for becoming more confident. Others wonder why Michael has not been murdered yet. In the mission, the players bank £13,500 and are told that they can choose three people to receive a special power. Unbeknownst to the Faithfuls, the players end up choosing all three Traitors (despite James A. saying he did not want the power). The power is revealed to be a dagger that represents a double vote at the Round Table. The Traitors choose to give the dagger to Kenny.

Because Sharon tried to avoid giving the power to James A., she falls under suspicion. Sebastian and Romesh think that James B. and Michael are influencing the group to banish Sharon. At the Round Table, suspicion falls on several players but primarily on Michael, Sharon, and Joanne. The vote is split and results in a tie between Sharon and Joanne. Sharon and Joanne plead their cases and the episode ends before Kenny's deciding vote.[32]
189"Episode 9"29 October 2026 (2026-10-29)TBD

Kenny switches his vote and Joanne is banished. The Traitors decide to murder Sharon to sow confusion. Realising he was wrong about Sharon, Michael decides to hone in on finding Traitors. Suspicions fall on James A. while Kenny and Sebastian suggest Michael could be a Traitor. James B. and Michael return to theorising on the murder in plain sight. Michael considers Maya and Kenny as Traitors who had teamed up. The players bank £5,000 in the mission. Romesh and Sebastian theorise Michael, James B., and especially Maya as being a Traitor trio.

At the Round Table, Michael leads the discussion and invites accusation to test all theories. Eventually, despite suspicions on Michael, Leigh-Anne, and James B., the discussion flips to the murder in plain sight. Michael delivers a direct accusation of either Maya or James A. murdering Ross, with James A. being the most likely culprit. Having been caught out, James A. confesses to being a recruited Traitor, and to his delight, he is banished with a unanimous vote. After the Round Table, suspicions continue to fall on Maya. She realises Michael is her biggest threat but that murdering him will only point back to her.[33]
1910"Finale"[34]30 October 2026 (2026-10-30)TBD

The Traitors choose to murder Michael because Maya knows she couldn't win if he remained. Sebastian and Romesh trust each other enough to finish the game together, while Leigh-Anne trusts Kenny and suspects Romesh. James B. suspects Maya and Kenny due to their presence at Ross's murder. In the mission, the players bank £18,500, bringing the final total in the prize pot to £83,000. Suspicion continues to fall on Maya, and Kenny doubts he can defend her without risking his own elimination. At the final Round Table, suspicion is split between Maya and Romesh, before Maya is banished with Kenny voting for her.

At the Endgame, all players choose to banish again. Leigh-Anne is banished for several reasons, including voting for Romesh with Maya, as this could have been a Traitor voting bloc. All players except Kenny choose to banish again. Sebastian and James B. suspect Kenny, while Romesh suspects James B. because he dismissed Maya's parting remark. The vote is split between James B. and Kenny. After deliberation, Sebastian and Romesh choose to banish Kenny.

Sebastian and Romesh choose to end the game, but James B. chooses to banish again because he now suspects Romesh for turning on him. James B. is banished. Romesh and Sebastian win the game as Faithfuls, splitting the prize pot and winning £41,500 each for their chosen charities, the Refugee Council and Choose Love respectively.[4]

Reception

Critical reception

Some outlets commented on the lack of sportspeople compared to the previous series, which included Tom Daley, Clare Balding and finalist Joe Marler.[18][35] In September 2026, The Times ranked Hesmondhalgh as most likely to win the series;[36] however, she was banished in Episode 4.[37]

The Guardian gave the final a 4/5 rating.[38] The Times described it as 'extraordinary result after a sometimes underwhelming series'.[39]
